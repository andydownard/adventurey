# Adventure-y: Jack's idea (2026-11-13)

Jack described the game out loud. This is his description, lightly cleaned up.

## Colors
Light green, light blue, and purple. The light blue and purple are the colors of the night sky. The green and the rest are the colors of the Northern Lights.

## The game
- The game is called **Adventure-y**.
- When you start, you come into a 3D forest.
- There are buttons in the forest. You click them, and they take you to a page where you choose:
  - Your pet: a few animals to pick from, like a baby dragon. Over the game you can evolve your baby dragon into a big dragon.
  - A bow and arrow or a crossbow.
  - Your first sword.
  - Your pack: a wolf pack or a dragon pack (and maybe others).
- Then you spawn at your pack's campsite with your friends, the other pack members.
- Monsters come out at night. Your pack has to protect the camp from them.

## Scope right now
Design only. No code yet.

## Design decisions (Jack, 2026-11-13)
- **Look:** realistic and 3D.
- **First sky:** night, with the Northern Lights.
- **Monsters:** cute spooky.
- **First screens to design:** the forest start screen, the dragon evolving, and the pack campsite at night.

## More ideas from Jack (2026-11-13, design review)
- Pick your pack (tribe) from the start screen.
- Make your own dragon: pick shapes for the body, wings, legs, tail and horns, or draw your own shape for any part.
- The pack members wear wolf hats (for the Wolf Pack), with arms and real clothes.
- A "Play with friends" circle, and a moon you tap to type your name. Any name works, even a made-up one like Wolf.
- Explore anywhere you want. Places include Aspen Forest, Cherry Blossom Valley, and the Ruins.
